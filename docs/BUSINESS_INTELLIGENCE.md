# ORPHEUS Business & Product Opportunity Discovery Framework

## Evaluation Matrix (Scoring 1-10 per dimension)
1. **Human Pain Point**: Is it a severe, recurring problem?
2. **Market Demand**: Proven willingness to pay or urgent global search volume.
3. **Competition**: Fragmented or weak existing solutions.
4. **Distribution**: Low-friction global/local channels (Gumroad, Projects.co.id, Etsy).
5. **Monetization**: High gross margin digital products (Excel templates, Notion packs, high-value guides).
6. **Difficulty / Maintenance**: Low technical debt, 'build once, sell repeatedly'.
7. **Capital Requirement**: Near zero upfront capital.
8. **Time-to-First-Revenue**: Fast execution (< 14 days).
9. **Risk**: Legal and platform compliance safety.

## Output Standard
Every business opportunity report must conclude with an **Action Plan**, **Estimated Revenue Potential**, and **Immediate Deliverable**.
