# ORPHEUS DIGITAL PRODUCT ENGINE (DP-Engine)
*Exclusive Framework for Digital Product Discovery, Validation, and Monetization.*

## 1. Core Philosophy: Start from Market Pain, Not Products
We NEVER start with "What product should I make?". 
We start with: **"What recurring, painful problem do people willingly pay money to solve instantly?"**

## 3. Opportunity Scoring Model (0–100)
- **Demand (15%)**: Search volume & buyer intent.
- **Pain Intensity (10%)**: Urgency and emotional/financial friction.
- **Willingness to Pay (10%)**: Direct correlation to buyer financial ROI.
- **Competition (10%)**: Inverse scale (lower competition = higher score).
- **Market Gap (10%)**: Distinctive positioning opportunity.
- **Production Difficulty (10%)**: Low technical barrier.
- **Time to Create (5%)**: Fast turnaround (< 7 days).
- **Distribution Difficulty (10%)**: Low friction global/local channels.
- **Profit Margin (10%)**: Near 100% digital gross margin.
- **Scalability (5%)**: Infinite digital copies without marginal cost.
- **Repeat Purchase Potential (5%)**: Upsell and cross-sell readiness.

### Scoring Labels:
- 🔥 **85–100 = HIGH PRIORITY**
- 🟢 **70–84 = PROMISING**
- 🟡 **55–69 = EXPERIMENT**
- 🔴 **<55 = REJECT**
